How we choose between repair and replacement

A repair makes sense when the cause is one failed part and the rest of the system is sound. Replacement makes sense when the same part fails again, when the equipment is past its normal life or when the repair costs most of a new unit. The plumber explains both options with prices.

Herriman adds a local factor. The city calls its water very hard, so equipment that touches hot water collects scale faster than it would in a soft water area. That tilts some decisions toward replacement and toward adding a softener afterward.
