Emergency Faucet Repair in Herriman, UT.

A faucet that drips, sprays sideways or refuses to shut off has a worn part we can usually name in minutes. Faucet repair in Herriman starts with the handle style, because a cartridge, a ball, ceramic discs and old compression washers all fail differently.

If the leak is under the sink or the shutoff valve will not turn, treat it as urgent. We repair what can be saved and replace what cannot, with the price agreed before we start.

Water spraying under the sink or at the wall

A broken supply valve or a snapped faucet line can flood a cabinet fast, and we dispatch 24/7 for it. Until a plumber arrives, your goal is to stop the flow and protect the cabinet floor.

  1. Close the stop valve.

    Turn the small oval handles under the sink clockwise. If only one line is leaking, shut that one.

  2. Use the main if the stop is stuck.

    A seized or broken stop valve means shutting the whole house valve instead. Do not wrench a corroded handle.

  3. Empty the cabinet.

    Remove cleaners and boxes, and lay towels or a shallow pan under the leak.

  4. Keep power away from the wet zone.

    If a disposal or outlet sits in the same cabinet, leave it unplugged until it is dry.

Take a photo of the leak before you clean up, since it helps with an insurance claim.

Which kind of faucet do you actually have?

Single-handle kitchen faucets usually hold a cartridge, a rubber-seated ball or a pair of ceramic discs. Two-handle faucets often use compression stems with rubber washers, or quarter-turn ceramic cartridges. The part that wears depends on that design.

Cartridge faucets drip from the spout when the O-rings or seals wear. Compression faucets drip when the seat or washer is damaged. Leaks at the base of the handle come from worn O-rings or loose packing nuts.

Bring up the brand and a photo of the handle when you call. It saves a trip for parts.

What Herriman hard water does to a faucet

The city lists local wells at 25 to 60 grains per gallon and the Jordan Valley supply at 10 to 12, which puts household water in the very hard range. Scale builds in aerator screens, spout cores and cartridge seats. That is why one faucet often loses pressure or spits long before it leaks.

A vinegar soak of the aerator works on light buildup, and it takes about a half hour. Heavily crusted aerators are cheaper to replace than to clean. Because Herriman water also swings with the season, a faucet that behaves in summer may clog in winter.

Try these before calling

Some faucet complaints do not need a plumber. Check these and call if the symptom remains.

  • Unscrew the aerator at the spout tip and look for white grit or debris..
  • Soak the aerator in white vinegar for 30 minutes, rinse and refit..
  • Check both stop valves under the sink are fully open..
  • Turn the faucet on and watch whether hot and cold both lose pressure or only one..
  • Tighten the handle set screw with a small hex key if the lever wobbles..

If water still trickles after the aerator is clean, the cartridge or a supply valve is the next place to look.

Shower valves, tub spouts and outdoor spigots

A shower that will not stay on one temperature often has a worn balancing cartridge. Replacing it takes a plumber twenty to forty minutes when the shower valve is accessible from the front or an access panel. A tub spout that drips after the shower starts has a failed diverter.

Outdoor hose bibs need their own care. Herriman advises residents to disconnect hoses, shut outdoor faucets where applicable and insulate exposed pipes before freezing weather. The city also recommends a vacuum breaker on every hose bib, which keeps lawn water from siphoning back into your plumbing.

How a faucet visit works

Every job follows the same path, whether it is a drip or a full swap.

  1. Shut off and inspect.

    We close the stop valves, open the faucet to relieve pressure and look at the stems, cartridge and seats.

  2. Name the part.

    You hear which part failed and whether it can be rebuilt.

  3. Quote before work.

    You get a clear price for repair and for replacement before tools come out.

  4. Repair or replace.

    New cartridge, seat, supply line or a full faucet goes in, with fresh plumber's putty or gasket.

  5. Test under pressure.

    We run hot and cold and look under the sink for weeping.

What changes the cost of faucet work

Faucet jobs range from a small seal kit to a new fixture and valve. These factors decide where yours lands.

  • Part versus whole faucetA cartridge or seal kit costs far less than a new faucet and the labor to install it.
  • Brand and finishPremium brands and specialty finishes carry higher parts costs. Some need special cartridges.
  • AccessTight sink bases, wall-mounted valves and tile surrounds take more time.
  • Valve conditionCorroded or seized stop valves are best swapped while the water is off.
  • Time of dayNight and weekend calls may be priced differently than a same-day appointment when available.

A sudden burst supply line that floods a kitchen is often covered, while a dripping faucet and gradual rot are typically treated as maintenance. Ask your insurer which applies.

Faucet questions, answered plainly

Do you handle faucet emergencies at night or on weekends in Herriman?

Yes. A burst supply valve, a sprayed cabinet or a faucet that will not shut off qualifies. Our line is answered 24/7, and we send the next available licensed plumber. We cannot guarantee an arrival time, so shut the stop valves while you wait.

Why is my faucet pressure so low?

Mineral scale in the aerator is the usual cause in Herriman. Soak or replace it. If pressure is low in every fixture, the issue may be upstream at a pressure regulator or valve.

What causes a faucet to drip after I shut it off?

A worn cartridge, damaged seat or old washer. Hard water speeds the wear because grit and scale scratch the seal faces.

Can you replace a bathtub faucet or shower valve?

Yes. Many shower valves can be repaired from the front by changing the cartridge. Valves with a cracked body need an opening in the wall and a full replacement.

Is a leaking shutoff valve an emergency?

If it sprays or runs steadily, yes. Close the main and call. A slow weep at the packing nut can wait for a daytime visit, but do not ignore it.

Will a water softener protect my faucets?

Softened water reduces scale that clogs aerators and cartridges. See water softener and filtration for how treatment is sized after you test your tap.

Do you also repair kitchen sink drains and disposals?

Yes. If the sink drains slowly as well, ask about drain cleaning, and for a jammed unit see garbage disposal repair.
